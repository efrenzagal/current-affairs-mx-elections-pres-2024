Mexico Federal Election Types

2024 General Election · INE PREP Data Reference

PRE — Presidencia de la República

1 race · nationwide · 6-year term

The presidential election. Voters choose one candidate by direct popular vote nationwide; whoever gets the most votes wins outright — there is no runoff. No incumbent can run again (absolute no-reelection rule). In 2024, Claudia Sheinbaum (Morena coalition) won with ~60% of the vote, becoming the first woman elected president of Mexico.

Voting methodPlurality (first past the post)
Seats1
Independents allowedYes
INE file suffixPRE_NAL_CAS
Notable columnsStandard party/coalition columns; no CAND_IND

DIP_MR — Diputaciones Federales · Mayoría Relativa

300 races · one per federal district · 3-year term

Single-member district races for the Chamber of Deputies (Cámara de Diputados). Mexico is divided into 300 federal districts, each electing one deputy by simple plurality — whoever gets the most votes in the district wins. Equivalent to a US House district race. Because voters choose a specific candidate (not just a party list), independent candidates are allowed here, which is why the data file has CAND_IND1 and CAND_IND2 columns.

Voting methodPlurality per district (FPTP)
Seats300 (one per district)
Independents allowedYes — appears as CAND_IND1, CAND_IND2
INE file suffixDIP_FED_MR_NAL_CAS
Notable columnsHas CAND_IND1, CAND_IND2 (all-NA in most districts)

DIP_RP — Diputaciones Federales · Representación Proporcional

200 seats total · 5 regions (circunscripciones) · 3-year term

Proportional representation seats for the Chamber of Deputies. Mexico is split into 5 multi-member regions called circunscripciones (numbered 1–5), each awarding 40 seats. Voters vote for a party list, not a person; seats are distributed proportionally by party vote share within each region. No independent candidates — you must be on a party list. This is why CIRCUNSCRIPCION is especially meaningful in this file. No party may hold more than 300 of the 500 total Chamber seats regardless of vote share.

Voting methodParty-list proportional representation
Seats200 (40 per circunscripción)
Independents allowedNo
INE file suffixDIP_FED_RP_NAL_CAS
Notable columnsCIRCUNSCRIPCION (1–5) is the key grouping

SEN_MR — Senadurias · Mayoría Relativa

96 seats · 3 per state · 6-year term

Each of Mexico's 32 states elects 3 senators by majority relative: 2 seats go to the winning party/coalition and 1 seat goes to the runner-up party/coalition (called primera minoría). This built-in minority representation distinguishes it from a pure winner-takes-all system. Senators serve 6-year terms.

Voting methodPlurality per state; 2 to winner, 1 to runner-up
Seats96 (3 per state × 32 states)
Independents allowedYes
INE file suffixSEN_FED_MR_NAL_CAS
Notable columnsStandard; state-level aggregation is the key lens

SEN_RP — Senadurias · Representación Proporcional

32 seats · single national list · 6-year term

Proportional representation seats for the Senate, allocated from a single national party list. Voters vote for a party; 32 seats are distributed nationally by vote share. Parties need at least 3% of the national vote to qualify. No independent candidates.

Voting methodParty-list proportional representation (national)

Independents allowedNo
